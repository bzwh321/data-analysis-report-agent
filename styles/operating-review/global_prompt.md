Use the `operating-review` page design style.

Design the report as a repeatable operating review tracker for weekly, monthly, or quarterly business reviews. The page should be fast to scan in a meeting: period and owner at the top, KPI status strip, exception queue, and action tracker. Use muted green as the operating color, amber for watch items, red only for exceptions, and gray for missing data.

Prefer ruled rows, compact status labels, tables, and short variance bars over decorative cards. The first screen must separate status, cause, and next action. Every action must include target, time window, owner role, dependency, and risk or evidence needed.

Use charts only when they reduce meeting time: sparklines, variance bars, exception rankings, or KPI status tables. Use direct labels and visible thresholds. Keep only decision-critical threshold notes near the relevant row; move ordinary data range, metric formulas, source lists, and chart notes to a concise end-notes section.

Use report-native labels in the report language. For Chinese reports, avoid generic English labels such as "Exception queue", "Action tracker", "Variance snapshot", or "Next check". Status labels are allowed only for actual states such as normal, watch, exception, and no-data. Do not use equal-width three-card summaries; operating reviews should end with next-check items, unresolved data gaps, or an action tracker.

Do not write a consulting memo. Do not use generic KPI card grids, gradients, large icons, or decorative dashboards. Do not include actions that cannot be checked in the next operating cycle.
